Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: 25 July 2026

ℹ️

This page explains our practices in plain language and is being finalized with legal counsel. It isn't a substitute for a signed service agreement — if anything here matters for a decision you're making, contact us at info@blactec.ug first.

This policy applies to anyone using BlacTec hosting, email, domain or cloud services. It exists to keep our infrastructure — and every other client on it — safe and reliable.

Not allowed

  • Hosting or distributing illegal content, malware, phishing pages or pirated material.
  • Sending unsolicited bulk email (spam) or operating open mail relays.
  • Attempting to gain unauthorized access to other accounts, servers or networks.
  • Running processes that abuse shared resources to the point of degrading service for other clients.
  • Using our services in a way that breaches Uganda's laws or the laws of a jurisdiction your content targets.

What happens if this policy is breached

Depending on severity, we may warn you, temporarily suspend the affected service, or terminate it — we'll always try to contact you first unless the issue is actively harming other clients or the wider network.

Reporting abuse

If you believe content or traffic from a BlacTec-hosted service is abusive, email info@blactec.ug with details and we'll investigate.
