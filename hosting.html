Control & protection

Run your cloud from your phone. Protect it your way.

Open the BlacTec client area on your phone like an app. Business Hosting and Professional Hosting add a built-in firewall and malware scan to keep your website safe.

  • Your control panel, on your phone

    Add the client area to your home screen and it opens like an app, with no app store. Check your services, pay invoices with Mobile Money and reach support from anywhere.

  • A firewall for each website

    Block or allow visitors by IP address or by whole country. Rules apply within seconds, and the tool warns you before you lock yourself out.

  • HTTPS-only and login protection

    Send every visitor to the secure version of your site with one switch, and limit repeated attempts on WordPress, Joomla and other common login pages.

  • Malware scan on demand

    Scan your website files whenever you like and see which ones match known malware. The scan only reports; it never changes or deletes your files.
